How to play

X starts. Take turns placing a mark in an available empty square. Three of your marks in a row wins a board. A full board without a winner is drawn.

Level 1 is one board. At higher levels, winning a smaller board claims its position in the board containing it. Win the outermost board to win the game. Won or drawn boards are closed. A diamond (◆) marks a drawn board.

Your move sends the next player to the matching board: at level 2, square 5 sends them to board 5. At level 3, position 2 / 5 / 7 sends them to board 5 / 7.

If that destination is closed, play opens within its nearest unfinished containing board. If the outer destination is closed, play opens across the whole board. The turn message shows where you can play.

Arrow keys move between available positions. Home and End move to the first and last available positions. Enter or Space plays.

Changing players or levels starts a new game.
