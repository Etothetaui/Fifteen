How to play

Claim relays. Redirect your opponent.

  1. Choose one available relay.

    You claim it with one move. Blue moves first; Red follows.

  2. Check where the move sends them.

    Your move determines their next location. The preview shows the exact destination before you commit.

  3. Claim three with the same emblem.

    Each relay carries several emblems. Claim all three relays sharing any one emblem. Select an emblem above the relays to highlight its three members. Winning a location claims one relay in its parent. The same rule continues through ten levels to the world.

When a destination has finished, play opens within its nearest unfinished parent. Available choices always come from the game rules.

You alternate one move each. Exploring and previewing cost no moves.

Patterned markers identify places. A place keeps the same marker when you return; markers are separate from ownership and emblem groups.
